# Annual Summit 2026 — Financing the hydrogen economy

Chatham House rule. Discussion on why hydrogen projects in North and East Africa struggle to reach financial close. Participants agreed that long-term offtake contracts and currency-risk cover are the main gaps. A Moroccan producer described a 400 MW electrolysis project seeking European buyers. A French research lead presented cost curves for solid-oxide electrolysis. Follow-up: Secretariat to connect producers with Northern European industrial buyers.
