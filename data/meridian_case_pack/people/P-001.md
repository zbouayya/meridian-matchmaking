# Victoria Hale

Victoria Hale is Chair & CEO at Atlas Logistics, based in Netherlands. Hale works on resilient trade routes, logistics and nearshoring strategies. They also has advised on hydrogen export corridors and ammonia shipping between producing and importing regions. They also structures blended-finance vehicles that crowd private capital into transition projects. They also builds skills pathways for young people entering a changing labour market. Victoria sits on several Forum boards and is one of the most connected members of the network; she is a frequent host at the Annual Summit. Last engaged with the Forum on 2026-10-21.
