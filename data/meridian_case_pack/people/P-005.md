Anders Lindqvist is Chief Executive Officer at Nordvolt Energy, based in Sweden. Lindqvist works on electrolyser deployment and long-term green hydrogen offtake agreements. They also has helped build traceable critical-minerals supply chains. Anders is preparing Nordvolt's entry into imported green hydrogen and is looking for producers and corridor partners outside Europe. Last engaged with the Forum on 2026-10-15.
