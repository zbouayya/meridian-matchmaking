# Dr. Marcus Feld

Dr. Marcus Feld is Senior Advisor, Hydrogen (Advisory Board) at Lumière Institute of Technology, based in Germany. Feld has advised on hydrogen export corridors and ammonia shipping between producing and importing regions. Marcus is widely regarded as one of the leading hydrogen experts in Europe and previously ran hydrogen strategy for a major utility. Last engaged with the Forum on 2026-10-20.
