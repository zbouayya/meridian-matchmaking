# Hydrogen Corridors Coalition

Builds bankable hydrogen and ammonia export corridors between North Africa, East Africa and European ports. Working groups on offtake standards and port infrastructure.

Participants: Fatima Zahra Benali (P-004), Anders Lindqvist (P-005), Hamza Rachidi (P-040), Dr. Samuel Mwangi (P-014), Victoria Hale (P-001), Yuki Sato (P-049).
